# Privacy Policy for LeadSnap Chrome Extension

**Last Updated: December 2024**

## Overview

LeadSnap ("the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension that helps freelancers capture and manage leads from websites. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection and Storage

### What Data We Collect
The Extension collects and stores the following information that you choose to save:
- Lead names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Company names
- LinkedIn profile URLs
- Website URLs
- Notes you add
- Tags you assign
- Lead status (New, Contacted, Proposal, Won, Lost)
- Source URLs (the webpage where you captured the lead)

### How Data is Stored
**All data is stored locally on your device** using Chrome's built-in storage API (`chrome.storage.local`). Your lead data:
- Never leaves your browser
- Is not sent to any external servers
- Is not shared with any third parties
- Is not accessible to us or anyone else

### Data You Control
You have complete control over your data:
- You can view all stored leads in the Extension popup
- You can edit or delete individual leads at any time
- You can export your data as CSV or JSON files
- You can delete all data using the "Clear All" button in Settings

## Permissions We Request

The Extension requests the following permissions:

1. **storage**: To save your leads locally in Chrome's storage
2. **contextMenus**: To add "Add to LeadSnap" option when you right-click selected text
3. **activeTab**: To read selected text and page URL when you capture a lead

## Data Sharing

We do **NOT**:
- Collect any analytics or usage data
- Send any data to external servers
- Share your data with third parties
- Track your browsing activity
- Access any data other than what you explicitly choose to capture

## Third-Party Services

The free version of LeadSnap does not use any third-party services.

The Pro version (when available) will use Stripe for payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data: https://stripe.com/privacy

## Children's Privacy

LeadSnap is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
