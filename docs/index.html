Privacy Policy

LeadSnap Chrome Extension • Last Updated: October 2026

Overview

LeadSnap ("the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension that helps freelancers capture and manage leads from websites. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

Key Point: All your data is stored locally on your device. We never collect, transmit, or have access to your leads or any personal information.

Data Collection and Storage

What Data We Store

The Extension stores the following information that you choose to save:

  • Lead names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Company names
  • LinkedIn profile URLs
  • Website URLs
  • Notes you add
  • Tags you assign
  • Lead status (New, Contacted, Proposal, Won, Lost)
  • Source URLs (the webpage where you captured the lead)

How Data is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.local). Your lead data:

  • Never leaves your browser
  • Is not sent to any external servers
  • Is not shared with any third parties
  • Is not accessible to us or anyone else

Data You Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • View all stored leads in the Extension popup
  • Edit or delete individual leads at any time
  • Export your data as CSV or JSON files
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data

Permissions We Request

The Extension requests the following permissions:

  1. storage: To save your leads locally in Chrome's storage
  2. contextMenus: To add "Add to LeadSnap" option when you right-click selected text
  3. activeTab: To read selected text and page URL when you capture a lead
  4. notifications: To confirm a saved lead and, in Pro, to remind you of a follow-up
  5. alarms: To show a Pro follow-up reminder at the date and time you set on a lead

Follow-up dates, deal values and addresses you enter are stored with the lead in your browser only, like every other lead field.

Data Sharing

We do NOT:

  • Collect any analytics or usage data
  • Send any data to external servers
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Track your browsing activity
  • Access any data other than what you explicitly choose to capture

Third-Party Services

The free version of LeadSnap does not use any third-party services.

LeadSnap Pro is sold through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe for payment processing. The extension asks ExtensionPay only whether you bought Pro. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data: stripe.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

LeadSnap is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR, CCPA, or other privacy laws. Since all data is stored locally on your device and we have no access to it, you can exercise all data rights directly through the Extension's interface.
