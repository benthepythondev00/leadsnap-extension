Privacy Policy

LeadSnap Chrome Extension • Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who we are

LeadSnap (Lead Capture & Simple CRM for Freelancers) is a Chrome extension made by Ben, an independent developer. You can find it on the Chrome Web Store.

In short: your leads stay in your browser. We have no server, no account system and no access to them.

What we collect

LeadSnap stores the following in your browser using Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • Leads you save: name, email address, phone number, company, LinkedIn profile URL, website URL, address, deal value, follow-up date, notes, tags, status (New, Contacted, Proposal, Won, Lost) and the source URL of the page you captured it from.
  • Settings: your preferences, including when you last exported a backup.
  • leadsSaved counter: a number counting the leads you have saved.
  • reviewAsk flag: records whether you answered the "rate LeadSnap" prompt ("later" or "done"). The prompt appears after your 5th saved lead, and once more at 25 if you chose "Not now". Then it stops.

All of this stays on your device. Nothing is stored in sync storage or on a server of ours. We do not use analytics, ads or tracking.

Why we collect it

  • Your leads and settings are stored so the extension can show, organize, export and remind you about them.
  • The leadsSaved counter and the reviewAsk flag make sure the rating prompt shows at most twice and then stops.

Permissions

  • storage: to keep your data in your browser.
  • contextMenus: to add the "Add to LeadSnap" right-click option.
  • activeTab: to read the selected text and page URL when you capture a lead.
  • notifications and alarms: to confirm a saved lead and, in Pro, to remind you of a follow-up at the time you set.
  • extensionpay.com access: so the extension can check whether you bought Pro.

Sharing

Your leads, settings, counter and flag are never sent anywhere. Nobody receives them, including us.

LeadSnap Pro is sold through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe for payments. The extension asks ExtensionPay only whether you bought Pro. Payment details go to Stripe, not to us; see stripe.com/privacy. The free version uses no third-party service. The Chrome Web Store handles installs and updates under Google's own policies.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Your leads, settings, counter and flag stay in your browser until you delete them or remove LeadSnap. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

Your rights

  • Access: you can view all your leads in the extension popup and export them as CSV or JSON.
  • Deletion: delete single leads at any time, use "Clear all leads" in Settings, or uninstall LeadSnap.
  • Contact: for any privacy question or request, email us at the address below.

Because we hold no copy of your data, you can exercise your rights under GDPR, CCPA or similar laws directly in the extension.

Children

LeadSnap is a tool for freelancers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and we hold no data at all, since everything stays in the user's own browser.

Changes to this policy

If LeadSnap's data use changes, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top.
