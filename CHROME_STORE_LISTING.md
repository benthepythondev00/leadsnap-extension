## Detailed Description (16,000 characters max)

```
LeadSnap - The Simplest Lead Capture Tool for Freelancers

Stop losing potential clients! LeadSnap lets you capture contact information from any webpage with a single right-click. Perfect for freelancers, consultants, and small business owners who need a lightweight CRM without the complexity.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Browse any website (LinkedIn, company pages, directories, etc.)
2. Select text containing contact info
3. Right-click and choose "Add to LeadSnap"
4. Done! Contact details are automatically parsed and saved

SMART CONTACT PARSING
LeadSnap automatically extracts:
• Names
• Email addresses
• Phone numbers
• LinkedIn profiles
• Website URLs
• Company names

No manual data entry needed - just highlight and capture.

PIPELINE VIEW
Track your leads through a simple Kanban-style pipeline:
• New - Fresh leads to follow up
• Contacted - You've reached out
• Proposal - Sent a quote or proposal
• Won - Closed deals
• Lost - Didn't work out

Drag and drop or use the status dropdown to move leads through your pipeline.

POWERFUL FEATURES
• Search - Find any lead instantly by name, email, or company
• Export - Download your leads as CSV or JSON
• Tags - Organize leads with custom tags
• Notes - Add context to each lead
• Source tracking - See where you found each lead

PRIVACY FIRST
• All data stored locally in your browser
• No account required
• No data sent to external servers
• Your leads stay yours

FREE TIER
• Capture up to 25 leads for free
• All features included
• No time limit

PRO VERSION
• Unlimited leads
• Priority support
• One-time purchase - no subscription

PERFECT FOR
• Freelance developers and designers
• Consultants and coaches
• Sales professionals
• Recruiters
• Small business owners
• Anyone who needs to track contacts

WORKS ON ANY WEBSITE
• LinkedIn profiles and company pages
• Business directories (Yelp, Google Maps)
• Company contact pages
• Conference speaker lists
• Portfolio websites
• News articles
• And more...

QUICK START
1. Install LeadSnap
2. Visit any webpage with contact information
3. Select the text you want to capture
4. Right-click → "Add to LeadSnap"
5. Click the LeadSnap icon to view your leads

SUPPORT
Questions or feedback? We'd love to hear from you!
Email: benthepythondev0@gmail.com

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED
• "Read and change all your data on all websites" - Required to show the right-click menu and capture selected text. We never read or store any data you don't explicitly capture.
• "Storage" - To save your leads locally in your browser.

Built with care for freelancers who want to focus on their work, not their tools.
```
